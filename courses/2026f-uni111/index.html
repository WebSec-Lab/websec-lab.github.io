Introduction to CSE

A first-year tour of computer science and engineering, from systems and HCI to AI and security, taught by the CSE faculty. Wednesdays 4:00–5:15 pm, Room 104-E206.

Computer science and engineering powers the technologies that are improving the quality of our lives, from deep learning to the networks and cloud computing that connect everyone and everything. This course introduces first-year students to the major areas of computer science, including systems, HCI, AI, software engineering, security, and data science, through one- to two-week introductory lectures with hands-on exercises, alongside alumni career talks and a faculty Q&A panel on students’ questions and the role of computer science in the AI era.

Attendance

Attendance is taken at every session through the UNIST Mobile Attendance System. In accordance with UNIST academic regulations, five or more absences result in an Unsuccessful (U). Attendance may also be manually re-checked at random during a session; leaving after checking in (a.k.a., 출튀) counts as an immediate U.

If you are unable to attend a session due to unavoidable circumstances (something beyond your control, not a matter of choice), provide supporting documents to the TA (Chaeyoung Moon) and the absence will be counted as attendance.

Notice
  • The Faculty Q&A Panel takes place on December 2 (Week 14). If there is anything you are curious about, from studying computer science to careers, submit your questions in advance.
  • We keep a participation score, and the top 3 students will each receive a 10,000 KRW 배달의민족 (Baemin) gift card at the end of the semester. You earn points when (1) you ask a question during class, or (2) the professor teaching that session acknowledges your contribution. To earn the points, after the class, email the TA (Chaeyoung Moon) with the professor of that session in CC, describing what you did in that class.
